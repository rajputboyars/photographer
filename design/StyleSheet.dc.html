Rules of the layout

Grid. 12 columns, 72px page margin on desktop, 20px on mobile, 20–24px gutters.

Corners. Nothing is rounded. Square frames, hairline #E3DACE borders.

Photographs. Full-bleed, no shadows, no filters. Captions sit on a dark gradient inside the frame.

One action. Every section points at “Check your date”. Secondary links never compete with it.
