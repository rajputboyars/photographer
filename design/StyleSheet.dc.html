Rules of the layout

Grid. 12 columns, 56px page margin on desktop, 20px on mobile, 20–30px gutters.

Photographs sit in prints. White card, 12px mat, hairline border, soft shadow, tilted 1.5–2.5° — alternate the direction so no two neighbours lean the same way.

Script is seasoning. One script phrase per heading at most, and never for body copy, buttons or navigation.

One action. Every section points at “Check your date”. Botanical line art appears at most twice per page, always behind content.
