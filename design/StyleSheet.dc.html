Rules

Grid. 12 columns, 44px page margin on desktop, 18px on mobile, 24px gutters.

Photos sit inside glass. 12px panel padding, 20px inner radius. Captions are small glass pills on top of the image, never bars beneath it.

Depth by offset. Cards in a row shift by ±24–40px so they read as floating at different distances.

Fallback. Where backdrop-filter is unsupported, panels fall back to a solid #141A2B at 96% — test it.
